QuickBooks Online wasn't built to consolidate your entities

QBO is one company per subscription. Your consolidation still happens in Excel. Keep the workbook and connect the books, live across every entity.

QuickBooks Online is a solid ledger for a single company. The moment you have a second file, then a fifth, then a fifteenth, the product you actually run every month is Excel.

QuickBooks Online does not produce a native consolidated P&L, balance sheet, or cash flow across company subscriptions. Each entity is its own set of books. So finance teams invent the same workaround: export entity 1, export entity 2, paste into a master workbook, remap accounts when someone renamed a class, and hope last month's formulas still point at the right file.

That grind gets worse every time you add an operating company, property, or fund.

What "consolidation in Excel" usually means

  • A folder of CSV or Excel exports that must be refreshed in order
  • Charts of accounts that drift between entities
  • Intercompany lines that only balance if someone remembers to eliminate them
  • A "live" workbook that was live on the day it was pasted

Even QBO Advanced multi-company tools that land data in Excel are still oriented around combined exports, not your permanent house format with partner schedules, management fees, or lender packs that recalculate when the books move.

Keep the workbook. Connect the books.

Genius Sheets sits above QuickBooks Online (and Xero). Simple formulas (GS.IS, GS.BS, GS.CF) pull live financials into any cell of the Excel or Google Sheets file you already own. Your formats stay yours. Refresh daily. Drill from a consolidated number down to the transaction. Connect every QBO file under one login.

That is the difference between "we exported into Excel" and "our consolidation model is the system of record for reporting."

What good looks like

  • One custom workbook that already matches your board or family format
  • Numbers that refresh when the ledgers move, not a monthly copy-paste cycle
  • Drill-down from any consolidated line back to the source transaction
  • Every QuickBooks Online entity connected under a single login

Built for multi-entity finance teams

Family offices, holdcos, PE roll-ups, and fractional CFO or accounting firms running many QuickBooks Online entities, not single-file shops. If you are managing 5 to 30 or more entities and living in Excel on top of them, this is the job we built for.
